01 · The offer

A home goes up

  • The landlord brings the property, and the truth of the listing.
  • FairRent gives it a place to be found.

02 · The match

A tenant comes into focus

  • Someone discovers the home and applies.
  • FairRent scores them in the open.
  • The landlord makes the final yes.

We show the picture. The landlord chooses the person.

03 · The meeting

They meet at the door

  • One shared calendar finds an hour that suits both.
  • The landlord walks them through the home.

We schedule the hour. FairRent does not attend.

04 · The money

Rent goes home

  • The tenant instructs a payment into the landlord’s own account.
  • That money never sits with FairRent.
  • If it is late, the landlord chases it, and can mark it here.

A bank moves the cash. The landlord watches it, and the arrears.

05 · The break

Something needs mending

  • The tenant raises it in the app.
  • An assistant checks the report and lines up a contractor.
  • The contractor prices the job.
  • The landlord approves the quote, and pays it.

We coordinate. They fund. A contractor does the work.

06 · The close

The record stays

  • The contractor finishes.
  • Landlord and tenant can say how it went.
  • FairRent keeps the cost, the time, and who did the work.

The line we do not cross

  • We do not walk the property
  • We do not choose the tenant
  • We do not write the lease
  • We do not hold or pass on rent
  • We are not the rent collector
  • We do not fund ordinary repairs
